## ⚠️ Notes to fix
1. ✅ *Fixed in the game.* **Regolith "3 to 5 meters"** (shelter info card): a study in *JGR Planets* (via Space.com) indicates that **1–1.6 m** of regolith would be enough to greatly reduce the dose. Suggestion: change to *"A layer of 1 to 2 meters of regolith already blocks much of the radiation."*
2. **Olympus Mons**: depending on how it's measured, sources give 22 to 26 km. "About 22 km" is fine, but if the judges ask, explain that it varies depending on the baseline used.
3. **0.84 kg of O₂/day**: this is the standard NASA figure, but the linked source is secondary.
